Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between title case and sentence case?

Title case capitalizes the first letter of every word (e.g. "How To Bake Bread"). Sentence case capitalizes only the first letter of each sentence (e.g. "How to bake bread").

Is this text case converter free?

Yes, completely free with no account, no watermark and no limit on how much text you convert.

Does it work with symbols and numbers?

Yes. Letters are converted, while numbers and symbols are left untouched.

Can I convert large documents?

Yes, paste as much text as you like. The tool runs entirely in your browser, so there is no size limit.
