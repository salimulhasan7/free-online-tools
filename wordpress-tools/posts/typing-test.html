What Your Score Means

  • Under 20 WPM - beginner, but practice makes perfect.
  • 20-40 WPM - average everyday typing.
  • 40-60 WPM - above average, good for most office jobs.
  • 60-80 WPM - fast and productive.
  • 80+ WPM - professional level.

Why Improve Your Typing Speed?

Faster typing saves you real time every single day - in emails, essays, coding and chat. Combined with high accuracy, it makes you more productive and less frustrated. Take this test once a week to track your improvement.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a good typing speed?

The average person types around 40 WPM. 60+ WPM is considered good, and 80+ WPM is professional level.

How is WPM calculated?

WPM (words per minute) = the number of characters you typed correctly divided by 5 (one word is roughly 5 characters).

Does accuracy matter?

Yes. Speed means nothing if you have to fix constant mistakes. Aim for 95%+ accuracy while you build speed.

Is this typing test free?

Yes, completely free with unlimited attempts. No account needed.
