Morse Code Basics

  • SOS is the most famous code: . . . - - - . . .
  • Letters are separated by a space; words by a slash or three spaces.
  • Dots are short beeps, dashes are long beeps.
  • Morse works for letters A-Z and digits 0-9, plus punctuation.

Why Learn Morse Code?

Morse code is history, but it is still alive - in amateur radio, emergency signalling and even as a fun skill. This translator makes it easy to write secret messages, decode movie scenes, or just impress your friends by blinking SOS.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I read morse code?

Dots (.) are short signals and dashes (-) are long ones. Combinations represent letters and numbers. For example, A is .- and B is -...

Does this translator support numbers?

Yes, digits 0-9 are fully supported along with the English alphabet and common punctuation.

Can I play the morse code as sound?

Yes, click "Play sound" and the code will beep through your device's speakers using the Web Audio API.
