Frequently Asked Questions

Is the coin flip truly random?

The result is generated by your browser's random number generator, which is designed to be unbiased. And just like a real coin, it's 50/50.

Can I pick from more than one option?

Yes, use the "Pick a Choice" tab and enter as many options as you like, one per line.

Is this free to use?

Yes, completely free with no account and no limits.

Does it work on mobile?

Yes, the tool is fully responsive and works great on phones and tablets.
