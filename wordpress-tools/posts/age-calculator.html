Frequently Asked Questions

How does the age calculator count months and days?

It converts your total time alive into years (using the average year length), then breaks the remainder into months, days, hours, minutes and seconds.

Does it count leap years correctly?

Yes, the calculation uses 365.2425 days per year, which accounts for leap years automatically.

Can I calculate someone else's age?

Yes, just enter their date of birth. There is no limit to how many calculations you can do.

Is my date of birth stored?

No. Everything is calculated locally in your browser and nothing is saved.
